IHF Triage

How to Play

Workload and Vigilance Simulator. Triage smart. Save lives. Beat the clock.

You are the triage clinician. Drag each patient to the right room before their timer runs out.

Three Steps

1
Press and hold a patient in the queue.
2
Drag to a glowing room that fits their priority.
3
Release. Staff treat them. Repeat.

Room Rules

Immediate to Trauma Bay
Urgent or Observation to Consult Room
Stable to Waiting Area

Levels Get Harder

Each level adds new pressure. Patients arrive faster, rooms move around the floor plan, room colours change, and cleaners block rooms between patients.

Win or Lose

Hit the level's patient target with few critical misses. Expired timers cost points.
